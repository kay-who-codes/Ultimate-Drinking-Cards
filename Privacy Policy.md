# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026
**Last updated:** 31 October 2026

## 1. Who This Covers

This policy explains how Ultimate Drinking Cards ("the App") handles data. The App is a static website served by GitHub Pages and run as a Discord Activity. Use of the App is also governed by the [Terms of Service](Terms%20of%20Service.md).

**The developer of the App does not operate a server, does not use analytics, and does not knowingly collect, store, or sell your personal data.**

## 2. What the App Itself Does NOT Do

- **No cookies** are set by the App.
- **No analytics or tracking scripts** (no Google Analytics, Plausible, etc.).
- **No accounts, no sign-ups, no email collection.**
- **No advertising.**
- **No selling or sharing of data** to anyone, because there is no data to sell.

## 3. Data Stored on Your Device

The App uses your browser's **localStorage** for two things only:

- Remembering which card categories you enabled/disabled.
- Remembering your Discord Mode preference.

This data **never leaves your device** and is not transmitted to the developer. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

## 4. Data Handled by Third Parties (Outside the Developer's Control)

Because the App is hosted on GitHub Pages and played inside Discord, those platforms process data independently:

**GitHub Pages (hosting)**
GitHub may log IP addresses, browser user-agents, timestamps, and requested URLs as part of normal server operation and security. GitHub is based in the United States; data may be processed there. See GitHub's Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

**Discord (Activity platform)**
When you use the App as a Discord Activity, Discord processes your account information, activity data, and interactions according to its own Privacy Policy. The App's developer does not receive or control this data. See Discord's Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

## 5. Territorial Scope and Legal Basis

The developer is established in the UK and is subject to the UK GDPR. The App is available worldwide, but the developer does not specifically target, market to, or monitor users in any particular country or region.

**The developer processes no personal data through the App.** No accounts, no analytics, no server logs controlled by the developer, no cookies, and no transmission of on-device preferences to any developer-controlled system. Because there is no processing by the developer, no lawful basis under UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or any other data protection regime is engaged on the developer's side.

The only personal data processing connected to the App is carried out by **GitHub** (hosting) and **Discord** (platform), each as an independent controller under their own policies (see §4). Users outside the UK should note that their local data protection laws may apply to GitHub's or Discord's processing of their data; the developer makes no representation about the adequacy or equivalence of those third parties' practices under any particular national law.

## 6. Your Rights

Since the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on the developer's side. For data held by GitHub or Discord, exercise your rights directly with those platforms:

- GitHub: https://github.com/contact/privacy
- Discord: privacy@discord.com

## 7. Children

The App contains alcohol-related content and is not directed at children. The [Terms of Service](Terms%20of%20Service.md) require users to be of legal drinking age for alcohol-related content in their jurisdiction.

The ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code ("Children's Code") applies to UK-relevant online services likely to be accessed by children, even where children are not the intended audience. A game hosted on Discord could in principle be accessed by under-18s. **However, the Children's Code applies to services that process personal data.** Because the developer processes no personal data through the App (see §5), the substantive standards of the Code are not engaged in respect of the developer's processing.

Users outside the UK should consult their local laws regarding children's access to alcohol-related content and online services.

## 8. International Transfers

GitHub and Discord are US-based companies. If you access the App from the EU/UK, your data may be transferred to the US under those companies' respective transfer mechanisms (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses, EU-US Data Privacy Framework). The developer makes no independent transfers.
